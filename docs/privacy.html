Privacy Policy

True North Baptist Church Bible Institute · Last updated October 1, 2026

“Providing for honest things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.” — 2 Corinthians 8:21

This site (tnbbibleinstitute.com) is the student and faculty portal of the True North Baptist Church Bible Institute, a ministry of True North Baptist Church. We collect only what is needed to run classes, and we never sell or rent anyone's information.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address. If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google shares only your name, email address, and profile picture with us. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else in your Google account.
  • Coursework — courses you enroll in, files and written work you turn in, grades, and the messages and discussion posts you write on the site.
  • Study Bible highlights — verses you choose to highlight, saved to your account.

How it is used

Only to run the Institute: to sign you in, enroll you in courses, let your instructors see and grade your work, deliver messages, and send account emails such as email confirmation and password resets. We do not use advertising or tracking tools.

Who can see it

Students see their own work, grades, and messages, plus what is shared with their class (such as discussion posts and course materials). Institute faculty can see student work, grades, and messages in order to teach and grade. Your information is not shared with anyone else, except as required by law.

Where it is kept

Account data and files are stored securely with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and account emails are sent through the church's Gmail account. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you read the Study Bible, the passage you open is requested from bible-api.com (no personal information is sent), and your browser may keep a copy of that passage to load faster next time.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to correct your information or to delete your account. Deleting an account permanently removes everything connected to it, including submissions, grades, and messages.
